# Code of conduct

This is a place to make documentation and examples better. Be plain, be kind and be specific.

- Criticise the text, the check or the claim, not the person. Say what is wrong and what would fix it.
- Do not harass, demean or exclude anyone, and do not post another person's private information.
- Say what you actually checked. A claim you could not check is labelled as such; an honest "I did not test this" is welcome.
- Keep to the purpose of the repository: documents, formats, manifests, examples and checks.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions and comments that break these lines, and may block repeat offenders. To report a conduct problem, open an issue that asks for a private channel and give no details in it; a maintainer will answer there.
